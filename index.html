Why kjournal

Ketamine-assisted psychotherapy (KAP) usually has three phases: preparation, the dosing session, and integration. Integration is where you make sense of what came up, but there's rarely a simple, structured way to capture it between sessions. I designed and built kjournal to fill that gap.

  • Session log. Date, dose, how you feel going in, your intention, and your reflection after.
  • Intention guide to help you set a focused goal before each session.
  • Sleep and habit trackers to spot patterns across treatment. Sleep is a useful thing to watch between sessions.
  • Open pages and looking-back reviews to bring to integration with your therapist.

Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. It's a journal for personal reflection. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Follow your care team's guidance about your treatment.

What kind of sessions is it for?

Any session you want to reflect on: ketamine or esketamine treatment, psychedelic-assisted therapy, talk therapy, or a hard week.

Where is my writing stored?

In your browser, on your device. We never see it. If you switch phones or clear your browser data, it won't carry over, so download a backup now and then.

Can I use it on my iPad with Apple Pencil?

Yes. The online journal supports Apple Pencil pressure. The printable PDF works in GoodNotes and Notability.

Is the online journal really free?

Yes, with no trial and no account. The printable version is the only thing for sale.
