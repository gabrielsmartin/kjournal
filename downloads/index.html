How to use it

  1. On an iPad: open the PDF, tap Share, then choose GoodNotes or Notability.
  2. On paper: print it. Print the session pages as many times as you need.
  3. Before your session: write one line on the intention page.
  4. After: write or draw what came up while it's fresh.
